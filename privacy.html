Last Updated: November 16, 2026

Statutory Transparency Notice: This Privacy Policy is published in strict compliance with Section 43A of the Information Technology Act 2000, Rule 4 of the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules 2011, the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 (DPDP Act), and the Google Play Store User Data Policy.

1. Operating Entity & Scope

This Privacy Policy governs the collection, processing, and protection of user personal data by HOME FOOD ("Platform", "we", "us", or "our"), operated by [LEGAL ENTITY NAME: e.g., Sagar Rathod Sole Proprietorship / Home Food Private Limited], with principal office located at [REGISTERED BUSINESS ADDRESS: Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India].

2. Categories of Data Collected

  • Mobile Phone Number: Collected during onboarding for passwordless authentication via One-Time Password (OTP) and transactional order status SMS dispatch.
  • Location Information (Precise & Coarse):
    • Customer: Foreground location (GPS) is accessed solely when the application is actively in use to identify nearby home chefs (within 2 to 5 km radius) and compute transparent delivery fees. Customer location is never tracked in the background.
    • Delivery Partner: Location coordinates are accessed during active delivery assignments to provide live tracking to the customer and navigation assistance.
  • Delivery & Order History: Delivery address (street, landmark, city, postal code), ordered dishes, dietary tags (Pure Veg, Jain, Farali), and chef ratings.
  • Financial & Payment Transaction Data: All online payment processing is executed via Reserve Bank of India (RBI) authorized payment aggregator Razorpay Software Private Limited. HOME FOOD does not store, process, or transmit sensitive debit/credit card numbers, CVVs, or UPI PINs. We store only anonymized order identifiers (razorpay_order_id, razorpay_payment_id) and transaction timestamps.
  • Regulatory Compliance Data: Home chef FSSAI registration numbers, food hygiene declarations, and delivery partner driving license details.

3. Purpose and Legal Basis for Processing

We process personal data solely for:

  1. Facilitating food orders between customers and independent local home kitchens.
  2. Dispatching delivery partners and computing accurate road distances.
  3. Issuing GST Section 9(5) tax invoices and maintaining statutory sales records under the CGST Act 2017.
  4. Preventing food safety disputes, duplicate refunds, and account abuse.

4. Data Sharing & Third-Party Processors

We strictly do NOT sell, rent, trade, or monetize personal data to third-party advertising brokers. Data is shared exclusively with:

  • Assigned Delivery Partners: Customer address and phone number for the active duration of the delivery trip.
  • Supabase Cloud Database: Enterprise cloud database (AWS Mumbai ap-south-1 region) secured with PostgreSQL Row-Level Security (RLS).
  • Razorpay: Payment aggregator for secure payments and instant refunds.
  • TRAI Registered Telecom Carriers: For transactional OTP and order notification delivery under approved DLT templates.
