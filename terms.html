1. Marketplace Platform Structure

HOME FOOD operates as a technology intermediary under Section 79 of the Information Technology Act 2000. We connect independent local home chefs ("Chefs"), independent logistics freelancers ("Delivery Partners"), and domestic customers ("Customers"). HOME FOOD facilitates digital ordering, discovery, payment collection, and customer support.

2. Food Preparation & FSSAI Compliance

All onboarding Home Chefs certify and warrant that:

  • They hold a valid FSSAI registration or license as prescribed by the Food Safety and Standards Authority of India.
  • They maintain domestic kitchen hygiene, clean water, and food-grade packaging.
  • Ingredients are fresh and meet all food safety standards.

3. Pricing, Delivery & Commission Model

All transactions on HOME FOOD adhere strictly to the following approved pricing schedule:

  • Food Price: Determined directly by the Home Chef.
  • Chef Net Earnings: The Home Chef receives 90% of the food subtotal (platform retains a 10% kitchen commission).
  • Delivery Fee: ₹40 base delivery fee (covers up to 2 km). For distances exceeding 2 km, an incremental rate of ₹8 per km applies.
  • Delivery Partner Earnings: The Delivery Partner receives ₹35 base earnings per delivery (₹40 delivery fee minus ₹5 delivery technology fee).
  • Platform Convenience Fee: A fixed ₹5 customer technology fee is added to each checkout.
  • Taxes: GST Section 9(5) restaurant service tax (5%) is collected and remitted to the Government of India in accordance with statutory requirements.

4. User Code of Conduct

Users agree not to misuse the platform, submit fraudulent refund claims, harass home cooks or riders, or circumvent the app payment system.

5. Limitation of Liability

As an intermediary marketplace, HOME FOOD is not the direct cook of the food or the direct employer of the delivery partner. While we enforce strict quality standards, FSSAI verification, and dispute resolution, our aggregate liability is limited to the transaction value of the affected order.
