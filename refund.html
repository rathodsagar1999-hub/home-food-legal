Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last Updated: November 16, 2026

Transparent Consumer Protection: This policy outlines the rules governing order cancellations, refund processing timelines, and dispute resolutions under the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules 2020.

1. Order Cancellation Policy

Because home-cooked food is prepared fresh in domestic kitchens using perishable ingredients, the following strict cancellation rules apply:

  • Phase A — Before Chef Acceptance:

    100% Free Cancellation. The customer can cancel immediately from the app. A full refund of the order amount, delivery fee, and platform fee is credited back instantly.

  • Phase B — After Chef Acceptance, Before Cooking Starts:

    Cancellation Request Only. Once the home chef has accepted the order and started procurement, an automatic free cancellation is not permitted. The customer may submit a cancellation request via the app, which requires mutual review.

  • Phase C — Once Cooking Starts (`cooking_started`):

    Strictly No Cancellation. To prevent wastage of freshly prepared homemade meals and protect home chefs from financial loss, orders cannot be cancelled once cooking has commenced.

2. Food Quality & Wrong Item Complaints

Customers are entitled to a full or partial refund in the following exceptional situations:

  1. Wrong Dish Delivered: The item delivered differs materially from the confirmed order.
  2. Food Quality / Spoiled Item: The food delivered is spoiled, unhygienic, or not fit for consumption. Photo evidence must be submitted through the app within 2 hours of delivery.
  3. Undelivered Order: The delivery partner fails to deliver the order to the customer's specified address.
  4. Spillage or Damage in Transit: Major packaging spillage caused during transit.

3. Refund Timelines & Payment Mode

  • Online Payments (UPI, Cards, Netbanking): Refunds are processed back to the original source account via Razorpay within 5 to 7 business days (depending on the issuing bank).
  • Cash on Delivery (COD): For verified quality complaints on COD orders, refunds are credited via electronic bank transfer (NEFT/IMPS) or platform credit upon verification.

4. Dispute Filing Procedure

To file a dispute, go to Profile ➔ Order History ➔ Select Order ➔ Request Refund / Dispute. Every ticket is assigned a unique tracking number (e.g., TKT-20261116-XXXX) and resolved within 24 hours.
