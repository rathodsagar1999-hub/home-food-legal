# HOME FOOD — Official Public Legal Documentation

This repository hosts the official, public-facing legal and regulatory policy documentation for the **HOME FOOD** mobile application and cloud services.

## Contents
- **index.html** — Legal Transparency Center Portal
- **privacy.html** — Privacy Policy (compliant with Indian DPDP Act 2023, IT Act 2000, and Google Play Store User Data Policy)
- **terms.html** — Terms & Conditions of Service (Intermediary Marketplace Rules)
- **refund.html** — Cancellation & Refund Policy
- **grievance.html** — Nodal Grievance Redressal Officer Information (Rule 5(9) Consumer Protection E-Commerce Rules 2020)
- **styles.css** — Mobile-first responsive styling
